About the shop

Why one shop, nine departments?

Our store is a "meeting place" — and that's the idea. Instead of visiting a separate shop for every errand, you pick up your groceries, drop off a xerox job, grab a birthday gift and try on a pair of sandals, all in one visit.

Family-run since 2010, we know our regulars by name and we know our shelves even better.
